Mandates servicing journey

Migrating a live, high-volume manual journey onto AI-enabled infrastructure

Mandates, the journey customers use to add, remove or update signatories on business accounts, is already live and processed largely manually today: around 730 requests a day (roughly 6,000 to 7,000 over a two-week period) across Business and Commercial Banking, currently supported by a team of around 70 people. Leading its migration onto a new AI-enabled strategic platform, phased by customer segment. On the new platform, an LLM-based classification layer and workflow orchestration engine route each request through OCR and signature verification, automated business-rule checks, and human review only where required. Not yet live in production. Platform-wide target once scaled: 70,000+ requests a month automated, with a 50%+ reduction in FTE requirement.
