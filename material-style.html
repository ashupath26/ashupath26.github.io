Mandates: a live, high-volume journey moving onto a unified AI-enabled platform

Mandates is how customers add, remove or update signatories on business accounts. It runs today, largely by hand, split across two disconnected legacy platforms. I am leading its migration onto one unified AI-enabled platform on AWS, phase by phase: government-backed securities customers first (single-party, then multi-party), then Commercial Banking incorporated entities, then every Commercial Banking flow, then Business Banking. No data migrates between platforms; a case that opens on the old platform finishes there. On the new platform, a rules engine works alongside LLM agents for business-name matching and persona verification, plus agents that pull live data from the UK Charity Commission, Scottish Charity Register and FCA's Financial Services Register, with human review only where the rules require it.
