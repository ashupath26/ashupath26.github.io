NatWest Servicing Business Platform: from Mandates to platform-wide automation

Mandates — the pilot for a platform-wide AI strategy

Customers add, remove, update or view signatories on their business accounts. Behind that simple request sits a fully AI-orchestrated workflow, classifying and routing every inbound request before a rules engine and orchestrator drives it end to end.

CRM email in→ Polaris (Claude Sonnet 4.5) classifies→ Camunda FluxNova orchestrates→ Textract OCR + signature match→ Adobe e-sign→ AI agent business checks→ ID&V / fraud / AML→ HITL review→ Fulfilment

Two-tier automation model

Incorporated companies
Full straight-through processing — signature verification plus automated Companies House lookup, no human touch.
Unincorporated companies
Full automation up to a final human-in-the-loop check.
